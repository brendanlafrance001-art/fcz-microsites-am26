California Sea Lion

General Information

Location

Western Coast of North and Central America

Habitat

Costal bays, Harbors, lagoons and river mouths

  • Scientific name:Phoca Vitulina
  • Taxonomy:
    • class: Mammalia
    • Order: Carnivora
    • Family: Phocidae
  • IUCN conservation status:Least Concern
  • Diet:Fish, Squid, Crustaceans, and other locally available marine prey.
  • Movement:The hind flippers and rear portion of the body provide major swimming propulsion.
  • Behavior:Less Vocal than sea lions, often resting quietly on rocks or beaches in smaller groups.
  • Adaptation:They can close their nostrils while diving and use sensitive whiskers to detect movement in the water.

About This animal

California Sea lions occur along the pacific coast of North America. They are eared seals, which means they have visible external ear flaps. Unlike true seals, they can rotate their hind flippers forward beneath the body, allowing them to move relatively effectively on land.
